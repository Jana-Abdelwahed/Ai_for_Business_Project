# Official Checklist for Starting a Business in Egypt (GAFI & Tax Rules)

## 1. Choosing the Legal Structure
* **LLC (Limited Liability Company):** Ideal for tech startups with co-founders. Protects personal assets. Minimum 2 partners required (or 1 partner via Single Person Company rules).
* **Sole Proprietorship (Fardi):** Managed by one individual. Simple to form, but unlimited personal liability for business debts.

## 2. Mandatory Documents for Incorporation
1. **Trade Name Certificate:** Issued via GAFI online or at GAFI center.
2. **Bank Certificate:** Confirming minimum capital deposit in an Egyptian bank.
3. **National IDs or Passports:** For all founders and named managers.
4. **Lease/Ownership Contract:** Valid property contract for company headquarters, notarized at the Real Estate Registration Office (Shahr El-Aqari).

## 3. Post-Incorporation Registration
1. **Commercial Register (Sijil Tijari):** Issued at GAFI or local Commercial Registry branch.
2. **Tax Card (Bitaqa Daribiya):** Issued by the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA).
3. **e-Invoicing / e-Receipt System:** Mandatory registration on the ETA Portal (`eta.gov.eg`) before issuing invoices.
4. **Social Insurance (GOSI):** Register company and employees with the Social Insurance Authority.
